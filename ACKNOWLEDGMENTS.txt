Scientific Background and Acknowledgments

1. Scientific Background & Key Literature
This software is informed by and built upon published research in penalized 
least-squares baseline estimation, convex optimization, and constrained 
proximal algorithms.

The author gratefully acknowledges the researchers for their foundational 
scientific contributions:

- arPLS Algorithm (Asymmetrically Reweighted Penalized Least Squares):
  Baek, S.-J., Park, A., Ahn, A., & Choo, J. (2015). Baseline correction using 
  asymmetrically reweighted penalized least squares smoothing. Analyst, 140(1), 250-257. 
  https://doi.org/10.1039/C4AN01061B

- ADMM Framework (Alternating Direction Method of Multipliers):
  Boyd, S., Parikh, N., Chu, E., Peleato, B., & Eckstein, J. (2011). Distributed 
  optimization and statistical learning via the alternating direction method 
  of multipliers. Foundations and Trends in Machine Learning, 3(1), 1-122.
  https://doi.org/10.1561/2200000016


2. Open-Source Ecosystem & Tools

the following foundational tools and libraries:

- Python Language & Runtime: Core programming environment.
- NumPy & SciPy: Essential libraries for high-performance numerical computing, 
  matrix solver routines, and scientific algorithms.

The author expresses sincere gratitude to all maintainers, developers, and 
contributors who make these invaluable tools available to the global scientific
